<p>CAPTCHA, short for <em>Completely Automated Public Turing test
to tell Computers and Humans Apart</em>,
is explicitly designed to prevent automation, so do not try!
There are two primary strategies to get around CAPTCHA checks:</p>
<ul>
<li>Disable CAPTCHAs in your test environment</li>
<li>Add a hook to allow tests to bypass the CAPTCHA</li>
</ul>
