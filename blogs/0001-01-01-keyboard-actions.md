There are only 2 actions that can be accomplished with a keyboard: pressing down on a key, and releasing a pressed key. In addition to supporting ASCII characters, each keyboard key has a representation that can be pressed or released in designated sequences. Keys In addition to the keys represented by regular unicode, unicode values have been assigned to other keyboard keys for use with Selenium.
